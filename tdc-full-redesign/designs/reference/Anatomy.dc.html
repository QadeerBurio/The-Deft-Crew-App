colour: 60 · 30 · 10.
60% warm paper is calmer than pure white and easier on eyes at night. 30% white cards and ink give structure. 10% yellow, only where an action or reward lives. When one thing is yellow, the eye goes there first (isolation effect).

type and space.
Only 3 text sizes on home: 26 for the hook, 19 for section titles, 14 for content. Outfit for headings (round and friendly like the logo), DM Sans for reading. 16px side margins and 24px between sections, so each block reads as one thought. Lowercase voice, short lines.
guardrails: fun, never pushy.
Pushes only for the 3 daily drops, never more. The streak is weekly, so missing a day costs nothing. No fake countdowns, no red badges. Every count shown is real, or it's hidden. Students should come back because it's good, not because they feel guilty.
